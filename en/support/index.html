Short answers for the Squish Attack iPhone app.

Frequently asked questions

How do I restore purchases?

Open Settings in the app and tap “Restore purchases”. You need to be signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy it. The purchase is a one-time €2.99 non-consumable (product squish.adfree) and stays tied to that Apple ID.

What does “Ad-free” actually include?

Ad-free removes ads before tools, the interstitial every five levels, and the endless-mode ad offer (every ten minutes). Each tool is still limited to once per attempt, and the star rules do not change.

Where is my progress saved?

Your progress lives on the device first. It is also backed up anonymously to the cloud when a connection is available. The player token is stored in the iOS keychain, so after a reinstall the cloud save can often be found again.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-in, no Game Center, and no Sign in with Apple or Google. You play anonymously.

I bought ad-free but I still see ads.

Tap “Restore purchases” in Settings and check that the same Apple ID is in use. Ad-free hides the ad formats listed above; it does not lift the tool limit. If it still looks wrong, get in touch.
