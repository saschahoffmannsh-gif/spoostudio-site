Privacy policy for Squish Attack

Last updated: 16 November 2026. This notice describes the current technical behaviour of the Squish Attack iPhone app. It is not legal advice. Remaining open points are marked. The German text at spoostudio.de/squish-attack/datenschutz is the primary version.

In short You play without an account, without a name and without an email address. We store a random device token and your progress. The iPhone app shows Google AdMob ads, and we ask for consent first. Optionally you can buy ad-free once through the App Store. There is no Game Center and no Sign in with Apple or Google.

1. Controller

Sascha Hoffmann, Hammelstrift 20, 67661 Kaiserslautern, email: spoostudio@icloud.com. Further details are in the imprint.

2. What data we process

Anonymous player token

When you first start the game, your device creates a random token. It stays on your device, primarily in the iOS keychain, with local storage as a fallback. Our server only stores a one-way value derived from it (SHA-256). That value cannot be turned back into an identity. We do not collect your name, email or Apple ID.

Save data and progress

So your progress can be backed up and restored on the same device, we store:

  • unlocked levels, stars and best move counts
  • your endless-mode score
  • the endless-mode tool inventory (rocket and wand)

In adventure mode each tool can be used once per attempt and refills on retry; tools do not accumulate. The saved record may still include inventory fields because that is how the current save format works.

Local tool log (test feature)

Settings include a tool-stats screen. It keeps a log on your device of when and where you used tools. You can export or delete it yourself. It is not sent to us automatically.

Duel

For the duration of a duel we store both players’ game state. Duel rooms are deleted after one hour at most.

Technical data for abuse protection

To limit request rates we process your IP address. What we store is only a shortened, salted SHA-256 prefix, which is deleted after two days at most.

3. Purpose and legal basis

The following legal bases are a draft and should be reviewed before the App Store release:

  • Providing the game, progress and duels: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (use of the game) and (f) (legitimate interest in a working, fair game).
  • Abuse protection: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.
  • Personalised advertising and tracking: only with your consent, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and § 25(1) TDDDG. Without consent the app shows non-personalised ads, relying on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, because ads fund the free game.

4. Retention

  • Endless-mode cloud saves are deleted if they have not been used for 90 days.
  • Adventure progress in the cloud (levels, stars, inventories) is deleted if it has not been used for two years.
  • Duel rooms: at most 1 hour after the last change.
  • Hashed IP values: at most 2 days.
  • Data on your device: until you delete the app. The keychain entry can survive a reinstall, so the cloud save can be found again.
Still open The database still holds an older leaderboard. It is turned off in the game. Whether and when those rows are deleted has not been decided. Individual entries can be removed on request via spoostudio@icloud.com.

There is no Game Center. Sign in with Apple or Google is not built in at present.

6. Advertising and consent (iPhone app only)

On first launch we first show Google’s consent form (UMP) to ask whether personalised ads are allowed. After that, Apple asks whether apps may track you across apps (ATT). If you decline, there is no advertising ID (IDFA); ads may still appear, without personalisation via that ID.

You can change this later: in iOS Settings under Privacy → Tracking, and, when Google requires it, in the game settings via “Change ad consent” / “Werbe-Einwilligung ändern”.

7. Ad-free purchase (iPhone app only)

In the app you can buy ad-free once. The product ID is squish.adfree, the price is €2.99, and it is a non-consumable StoreKit item. The purchase removes ads before tools, the interstitial every five levels, and the endless-mode ad offer (every ten minutes). The “each tool once per attempt” limit and the star rules stay in place. You can bring the purchase back in Settings via “Restore purchases” on a device signed in with the same Apple ID.

8. This website

spoostudio.de is served via GitHub Pages. These pages do not set our own cookies and do not use analytics tools. The host processes your IP address in logs when you visit. Fonts are hosted on this server, not via Google Fonts.

You may lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, for example the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information of Rhineland-Palatinate (Landesbeauftragter für den Datenschutz und die Informationsfreiheit Rheinland-Pfalz), Hintere Bleiche 34, 55116 Mainz (datenschutz.rlp.de).

Finding your data Because we do not know your name and the game does not currently show a visible player ID, there is no self-service lookup yet.
